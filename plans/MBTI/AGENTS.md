## Never do this

- **Never `write`/`edit` a `.docx` as text.** It is a zip binary; overwriting it destroys the document. Use `python-docx` (v1.2.0 is installed, Python 3.14.7).
- Don't silently reformat: the body is deliberately paragraph-only. If you add scoring tables you are changing the doc's structure — say so.
- Only **3** real Word styles are in use: `Title` (line 0), `Heading 1` at `Phần 1. Sở thích nghề nghiệp`, `Heading 2` at `Thực hành và kỹ thuật`. The other five RIASEC group titles and `Phần 2` are plain body paragraphs, so there is almost no outline to preserve — if you regenerate the file, match that; don't "improve" it by promoting everything to headings.
- Don't claim the self-written items are validated. `noidung.md` explicitly states no source confirms the reliability/validity of these custom questions, and that O*NET's psychometrics do not transfer. Keep that disclaimer in any rewritten text.
- Don't copy the credentials from `../nhap.txt` into any document, commit, or report.
- Don't copy official MBTI Step I/II items, scales, or report text — the docs use those sources only to organise reflection themes.

## Verified content contract (from the JPGs — keep these numbers exact)

**Part 1 — Holland/RIASEC**, items `01`–`60`, 5-point scale (1 Rất không thích … 5 Rất thích):

| Nhóm | Câu hỏi | Tổng |
|---|---|---|
| R | H01–H10 | /50 |
| I | H11–H20 | /50 |
| A | H21–H30 | /50 |
| S | H31–H40 | /50 |
| E | H41–H50 | /50 |
| C | H51–H60 | /50 |

Groups are contiguous blocks of 10 in document order. Sums fall in 10–50, **no cutoffs**; rank high→low, keep ties, highlight 2–3 groups. Unanswered ≠ 0.

**Part 2 — four MBTI-style pairs**, 48 items, scale −2…+2, 12 items per pair. `D = Σ(even-numbered items) − Σ(odd-numbered items)`, range −24…+24; positive leans to the first letter (E, S, T, J), negative to the second (I, N, F, P); `D = 0` = no side, keep the conclusion open.

| Cặp | Chẵn | Lẻ |
|---|---|---|
| E–I | 02 04 06 08 10 12 | 01 03 05 07 09 11 |
| S–N | 14 16 18 20 22 24 | 13 15 17 19 21 23 |
| T–F | 26 28 30 32 34 36 | 25 27 29 31 33 35 |
| J–P | 38 40 42 44 46 48 | 37 39 41 43 45 47 |

Word does **not** auto-total these; they are hand/calculator-scored. `D` is a response-lean difference, not a strength score — preserve that wording.

## Reading the docx (recipe that works here)

`python-docx` is the reliable path; ad-hoc XML stripping produced inconsistent results.

```powershell
python -c "import docx; d=docx.Document('MBTI.docx'); [print(p.text) for p in d.paragraphs if p.text.strip()]"
```

To inspect structure/regenerate:

```powershell
python -c "import docx; d=docx.Document('MBTI.docx'); print(len(d.paragraphs), len(d.tables), len(d.sections))"
```

**Encoding gotcha:** the Windows console here defaults to `cp1258`, so printing Vietnamese to stdout crashes with `UnicodeEncodeError`, and `Out-File`/`Get-Content` round-trips mangle diacritics. Write extracted text with an explicit BOM-less UTF-8 writer (`[IO.File]::WriteAllText($p,$s,(New-Object System.Text.UTF8Encoding($false)))`) or read files back with `encoding='utf-8'`.
